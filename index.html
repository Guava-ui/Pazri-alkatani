Hello there

I'm Pazri Alkatani, a Full Stack Web Developer and Computer Science student who is passionate about turning ideas into real, working products. I started my coding journey by building simple landing pages — and quickly fell in love with the entire process of crafting web applications from front to back.

On the front-end I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React and Next.js to create clean, responsive and accessible interfaces. On the back-end I build robust APIs with Node.js, Express, MongoDB and MySQL.

Outside of coding, I enjoy learning new things, contributing to projects like books, and sharing what I learn with the developer community.
